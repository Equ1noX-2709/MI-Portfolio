Interpersonal Intelligence

I demonstrate my interpersonal intelligence through the relationships I build and the way I understand and interact with the people around me. I value my friendships, especially the bonds I share with my friend groups, Bastea and KKK, and my best friend, Paul. I believe that meaningful relationships are built on trust, understanding, and genuine care for one another.

Although I may seem reserved at first, once people get to know me and I become comfortable around them, I can be an easy person to talk to. I consider myself a good listener because I value what others have to say and try to understand their perspectives before responding. I also believe I am good at reading the room, allowing me to recognize the atmosphere of a situation and pick up on social cues. This helps me become more aware of how others might be feeling and how I should respond appropriately. Above all, I strive to be a genuine, trustworthy, and loyal friend who values the people in my life.
